YTU Backup - Privacy Policy

YTU Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses. With the owner's permission, it reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive account, so that it can keep a backup copy of the owner's files there.

What it collects. Nothing. It stores no personal information, keeps no records of any other person, and uses no analytics.

What it shares. Nothing. No data is sent to anyone other than Google Drive itself, in the owner's own account.

Access tokens. The Google sign-in token stays on the owner's computer. The owner can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
